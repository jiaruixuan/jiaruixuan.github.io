About Me

I am Ruixuan Jia, an undergraduate student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. My research interest lies in large language models and agentic systems, especially how intelligent systems can reason under structured context, interact with external tools, and adapt to dynamic environments.

My research so far has mainly focused on three connected themes: test-time reasoning and inference in language models, real-world interactive agents, and the gap between controlled benchmarks and realistic environments. Although some of my recent work has been conducted in software development settings, my core interest is not limited to code generation itself.

An Empirical Study of Proactive Coding Assistants in Real-World Software Development

Lehui Li*, Ruixuan Jia*, Guo-Ye Yang, Jia Li.

arXiv preprint, 2026. Equal Contribution.

arXiv

We study the simulation-to-reality gap in proactive coding assistants through large-scale real-world IDE interaction traces. The work collects approximately 4.63 million operation events from 1,246 experienced industry developers over three consecutive days, constructs ProCodeBench for proactive intent prediction, and analyzes how real-world data, LLM-generated simulated data, and mixed-data training regimes affect model performance.
